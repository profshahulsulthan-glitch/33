Naan Mudhalvan is a flagship skill development initiative launched by the Government of Tamil Nadu to empower students and youth with industry-relevant skills. The program aims to bridge the gap between academic education and employability by providing structured training, career guidance, and certification opportunities. It focuses particularly on preparing school and college students for competitive careers in emerging fields such as information technology, artificial intelligence, entrepreneurship, and communication skills.

The initiative was launched in 2022 under the leadership of M. K. Stalin. Through this program, students are given access to curated learning modules, aptitude assessments, soft skill training, and placement support. Naan Mudhalvan also collaborates with industries and educational institutions to ensure that the training content matches current market demands.

One of the major strengths of Naan Mudhalvan is its digital learning platform, which allows students to register, choose career paths, and track their progress. The program also conducts workshops, boot camps, and mentoring sessions to enhance practical knowledge and confidence among learners.

Overall, Naan Mudhalvan plays an important role in improving the employability of youth in Tamil Nadu by nurturing talent, promoting skill-based education, and guiding students toward successful career opportunities.
